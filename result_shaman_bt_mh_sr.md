Three of the five are the best reserves on the entire combined list — Deadly Cuffs and Band of
Devastation are the two highest-value items available to this shaman across both instances, and
nobody else touched them. The other two are where the value leaks:

- **Madness of the Betrayer is a six-way roll in this raid.** Two Fury Warriors (Middle, Pøtçh),
  an SV Hunter (Mupke) and a BM Hunter with a double reserve (Tiptoptove) are all on it. A 1-in-6
  shot at +39.3 is worth less than a sure +13.4.
- **Softstep Boots of Tracking is a weak fifth.** +13.0 in a slot where the raid left nothing
  better uncontested, while Mantle of Darkness (+21.0, shoulder) sits unreserved.

five picks were free wins.
